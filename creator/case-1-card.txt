Starter-Home Build Challenge — The Sims 4
Created by Melissa

Goal: Build a livable starter home in 60 minutes, for §20,000, with exactly one offensively orange room.

Fixed rules:
- 60-minute speed build — build stops when the timer ends.
- Budget: §20,000 total, house + furnishings.
- Base game only — no packs, no CC, no mods for the build.
- No money cheats — no motherlode, rosebud, kaching, or money X. Start fresh: a new household starts at §20,000.
- Exactly one room must be offensively orange. One room only.
- Next host picks the color.

Choices left to the host:
- Lot: pick the lot and lot size — same lot for everyone.
- Next host: decide who hosts next — the winner, or rotation.
- Voting: decide how voting works and what you vote on — for example livability, budget, and orange commitment — and whether players can vote for their own build.
- Proof: decide what proof players share — for example a final budget screenshot and a screenshot of the orange room.
