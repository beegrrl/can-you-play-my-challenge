# Step 0: what Muse can hand over

Checked on November 24, 2026, in Muse for macOS, version 13.1, on the Free plan.

## The short answer

A challenge travels as pasted text. Muse can also build a card inside a chat, and the card carries its own line of text to copy.

## What the app showed

- **Chats.** An account has 1 main chat and any number of side chats. A side chat is a separate thread, and Muse names it after the first message.
- **Invite.** The Invite button offers a referral code for joining Muse. It does not bring a second person into a chat.
- **Memory.** The General and Data controls sections of Settings have no switch for turning memory off, and no private chat. Data controls offers Import memory, Export your information and Reset Muse.
- **Ideas and Feed.** Ideas lists what Muse offers to do for the account owner. Feed is a personal news feed. Neither has a control for sharing a challenge.
- **Early access.** This account is not in Meta's early access program for Muse. The app and its Mac version are public releases.
- **Copying.** Each text answer has a Copy response button. Select All takes both sides of a chat as text. A card is selected as 1 block, so the test asks Muse for each card again as plain text.

## What a card looks like

A throwaway prompt asked for a sock drawer challenge with a 10-minute limit and 1 odd pair, plus a card for a host. Muse built the card inside the chat in about a minute. The card is a small working page with a title, a host checklist, 3 steps and a timer. Its first block is labeled as the brief, with the words "copy this to remix it".

Muse also added to the challenge. It set the number of pairs and banned hints until 5 minutes were left. It wrote win tiers too. Then it asked whether to make the odd pair harder or easier.

## What Muse says about sharing

Asked on November 24 what a second user needs, Muse answered in its own chat. This is its account, and the publish step has not been tested.

- The smallest handoff is the challenge brief as a paragraph of text. A published Idea card carries the same text in 1 tap.
- A static Space can be published to a public link. It needs approval for each update and carries only the page as it looks.
- Memory belongs to the account. It follows the owner into a fresh chat. It does not travel to a second user through a Space, a link or an Idea.

## Not checked

- What a signed-out visitor sees at a published link. No handoff in this test uses a link.
